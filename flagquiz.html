How to play

  • A flag appears. Name the country it belongs to.
  • Choose: pick the right country from four. Watch out, the wrong answers are often from the same part of the world, and sometimes flags that look nearly the same.
  • Type: type the country's name. Spelling doesn't have to be perfect, accents don't matter, and short names like USA, UK and DRC count. You get three tries. Hint shows the next letter; Skip gives up on that flag.
  • Pick one or more continents, or the whole world, and how many flags to do.
  • Get flags right in a row to build a streak.
  • Flags you get wrong go into Mistakes, so you can practise just those. Get one right twice and it leaves the list.
  • The Flag atlas shows every flag with its name, for learning. A green dot means you know it well.
