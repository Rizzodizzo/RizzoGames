How to play

RunHold the joystick on the left side and push left or right.
JumpTap the right side. Hold for a higher jump. Touch a wall and you can jump off it too.
DashSwipe the right side: you dash that way, in any of 8 directions.
Dash jumpJump while you’re dashing along the ground and you fly off at full speed.
Get it backOne dash at a time. Land on the ground, or grab a gold orb or a spring, to get it back. Walls give you a jump, not a dash.
