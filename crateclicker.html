Help Bot

BEEP BOOP! Welcome aboard. Here's how this ship works.

  • Tap the crate on the pad to earn points and crack it open. Every tap counts, so tap fast (use more than one finger if you like).
  • Crates come in six rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic. Rarer crates take many more taps, but hold better items and pay out more.
  • Every item gives a boost: more points per tap, automatic taps every second, critical hits (5× taps), stronger taps (crates open quicker), luck (rarer crates) or a points multiplier.
  • Duplicates level up that item. Each copy fills its bar, and a full bar raises its tier, which makes its boost bigger. Higher tiers need more copies.
  • Most crates are standard cargo crates with one item. Now and then a Choice pod (pick one of three items) or a Multi stack (four items) turns up. The Fabricator builds those and more: Lucky orbs, Copycat canisters and Mega containers.
  • Crates get tougher. Every 25 crates you open, the crate level goes up and crates need more taps (and pay out more).
  • Every new crate level gives you a gem (two for a new best). Spend gems in the Power Lab on power-ups, and check the Trade Post for new one-time deals every day.
  • When it gets slow, go to the Star Map and prestige. You start again from crate level 1 with no items, but you earn stars, and every star makes your taps and points stronger for good.
  • Automatic taps keep earning points (at half speed) while you're away, for up to 4 hours.
